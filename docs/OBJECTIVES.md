# Objectives – Smart Farming AI

## 1. Main Objective

The main objective of Smart Farming AI is to develop a web application that helps farmers make better farming decisions using modern technology and AI.

## 2. Specific Objectives

- To suggest suitable crops based on soil conditions.
- To provide information about soil nutrients.
- To recommend suitable fertilizers.
- To provide weather updates.
- To help identify plant diseases.
- To make farming information available in one place.
- To provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
- To store user details and previous farming records.

## 3. Project Goal

Our goal is to make farming information easier to access and help farmers use technology for better agricultural planning.
